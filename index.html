01 / ABOUT US

Quality meals.
Local impact.

We believe everyone deserves access to a good meal, whatever they are going through.

Wellcare Foundation aims to strengthen Australian communities through quality meals, emergency food relief and compassionate community support. We welcome children, families, adults, seniors and people with disability, with dignity and respect at the heart of our approach.
